Three generations, one shop on Kasturba Road

MM Mithaiwala opened its doors in Malad in 1976, a single counter of kaju katli and motichur laddu run by hand. What started as one man's recipe book became a Malad institution passed down through three generations — the same sweets, the same slow methods, made for a neighbourhood that grew up on them.

Fifty years on, we wanted to mark the anniversary the way we've always done things: by hand, with something you keep rather than something you throw away. The Golden Collection is that — a gift box built to outlast the gifting.

One box, two lives

The sleeve that holds your mithai during the gifting is the same sleeve that becomes a lantern once it's empty. No extra packaging, nothing to throw away — just fold along the score lines and set a tea-light inside.

  1. Arrives A sealed jaali-lattice box of mithai, ribbon-tied, ready to gift.
  2. Opens Twelve (or nine) pieces, hand-arranged, with the sleeve doing double duty as the box.
  3. Folds Empty the box, fold the laser-cut cardstock along its creases, and it stands as a lantern — add a tea-light and it's yours to keep.
